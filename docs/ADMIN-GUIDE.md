# Your website admin: a quick guide

Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** and sign in with your username (or email) and password. It works on your phone as well as your computer.

> Forgotten your password? Whoever set up the site can reset it with one command (see the README). Nobody else can
> create an account. After 10 wrong passwords in a row, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes to stop guessing attacks.

## Overview

The first screen shows how many tattoos are published, how many are drafts, how many carousel slots you've used, and
any new enquiries. The four large buttons take you to the most common tasks.

## Upload a new tattoo

1. **Tattoos → Upload tattoo** (or *Upload a new tattoo* on the Overview).
2. **Add the photograph.** Tap *Choose a photograph*, or drag the file onto the box. Use the best-quality JPEG, PNG or
   WebP you have, up to 15 MB. The website makes an optimised copy automatically. iPhone photos in HEIC format need to
   be shared or exported as JPEG first.
3. **Title.** A short name visitors will see.
4. **About this tattoo** *(optional)*. The idea, the story, or how it was made.
5. **Size.** Small, Medium or Large. Visitors can filter the portfolio by size.
6. **Estimated starting price** *(optional)*. Just the number, in rupees, e.g. `12000`. It appears as "From ₹12,000"
   with a note that the final price is confirmed at consultation. Leave it empty and no price is shown.
7. **Style** and **Placement** *(optional)*, e.g. "Fine line", "Inner forearm".
8. **Image description** *(optional)*. One sentence describing the photo for blind visitors who use screen readers.
9. **Published.** Leave it on to show the tattoo straight away, or turn it off to save a private draft.
10. **Feature on homepage.** Tick it to add the tattoo to the big homepage carousel.
11. Press **Upload and publish** (or **Save draft**). A progress bar shows the upload.

## Edit, hide or delete a tattoo

**Tattoos** lists everything, newest first. Search by name, or filter by size and by published/draft.

- **Edit**: change any detail. *Replace photograph* swaps the picture; the old one is deleted when you save.
- **Hide it**: open it, switch **Published** off and save. It disappears from the website (and from the homepage
  carousel) but isn't deleted.
- **Star**: adds a published tattoo to the homepage carousel, or takes it off.
- **Delete** (bin icon): removes the tattoo and its photo for good. You'll be asked to confirm.

## Choose the homepage carousel

**Homepage carousel** controls the large slideshow at the top of your homepage.

- **Right side:** all your published tattoos. Tap one to add it (a tick and its position number appear), tap again to remove it.
- **Left side:** the carousel in order. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder, or use the ↑ ↓ arrows. × removes a tattoo.
- **Preview:** shows exactly how the carousel will look.
- Nothing changes on the website until you press **Save** in the bar at the bottom. *Discard* undoes your changes.

The carousel holds up to 7 tattoos by default. You can change that under **Website settings → Homepage carousel**.
If nothing is selected, the homepage shows your most recent tattoo instead.

## Enquiries

Messages sent through the website's contact form appear here, with the person's idea, preferred size, placement and
how they'd like to be contacted. Use the WhatsApp, phone and email buttons to reply, then press **Mark as replied** so
the enquiry moves out of *New*.

## Website settings

Change the words and contact details shown across the site:

- **Name:** your brand/studio name (shown in the header), your own name and a one-line tagline.
- **Homepage:** the short introduction beside the carousel.
- **About you:** your portrait, biography, philosophy, experience and the styles you work in. Leave a blank line
  between paragraphs. Any box left empty is simply hidden on the site.
- **Contact:** WhatsApp number (with country code, e.g. +91 98765 43210), phone, Instagram (@handle is fine),
  email and studio location. These power every WhatsApp button on the site.
- **Process steps & FAQs:** the steps on your homepage and Process page, and the questions on the FAQ.
  **These start with general example text. Please read them and rewrite them in your own words.**

Press **Save settings** at the bottom. Changes appear on the website straight away.

## Tips

- Photographs taken in soft, even light, with the tattoo filling most of the frame, look best in the gallery.
- Portrait (vertical) photos suit the layout best.
- Remember to sign out on shared computers (*Sign out* at the bottom of the menu).
